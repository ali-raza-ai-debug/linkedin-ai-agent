Most people think setting AI temperature to zero completely stops hallucinations.

It actually does not.

When we set temperature to 0, the model becomes deterministic.
It always picks the most likely next word.

But research shows this can trap the model in repetitive loops.
It gets stuck in high-probability word patterns that it cannot escape, which can actually cause errors.

Another big cause of hallucinations is the context window limit.

When a long conversation exceeds this limit, earlier details drop out of the working memory.
Instead of saying it forgot, the AI tries to fill the gap with plausible-sounding guesses.

And if you raise the temperature too high, the problem gets much worse.
Recent tests on the Qwen3-14B model showed that raising the temperature from 0.3 to 0.9 caused the hallucination rate to jump from 22 percent to 72 percent.

Imagine taking an exam when you already have all the exact questions. 💡

You would get a top score, right?

But it wouldn't prove that you actually understood the subject.

That is why we split our data into training and testing sets in Machine Learning.

When learning ML, this simple step turned out to be one of the most crucial concepts.

The training set is like your textbook. The model uses it to learn underlying patterns.

The test set is the final exam. It uses unseen data to check if the model can handle new information.

If a model just memorizes the training data, it overfits and fails when introduced to real-world data.

However, how we split the data is just as important as the split itself.

For example, if you split customer data purely at random, orders from the exact same customer could end up in both sets.

This creates a hidden problem called data leakage.

The model appears accurate simply by recognizing repeat customers, rather than learning actual buying behavior. 📊

Proper splitting helps us evaluate our models honestly before deploying them.

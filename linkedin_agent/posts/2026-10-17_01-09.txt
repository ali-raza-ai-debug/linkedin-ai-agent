Most people think machine learning is magic. 🤖

It is actually much simpler than that.

In traditional programming, you write the rules yourself.

Data + Rules = Output.

If you want to detect a cat in a photo, you would have to code rules for ears, whiskers, and tails. 🐱

That gets exhausting very fast.

Machine learning flips this completely.

Data + Output = Rules.

Instead of writing rules, you show the computer thousands of cat photos and let it find the patterns.

The computer learns from experience, just like humans do.

When I started my ML journey, this concept changed how I look at software. 💡

We are no longer just writing instructions. We are teaching systems how to learn.

Now as I explore LLMs and AI agents, this simple idea remains the foundation of everything.

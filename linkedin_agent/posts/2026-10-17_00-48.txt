Traditional coding is like giving a computer a strict recipe. 

Machine Learning is completely different.

Here is the simplest way I learned to understand it:

In normal programming, you feed the computer two things:
1. Data
2. Rules

And it gives you the Output.

For example:
Data: Item price is $10.
Rule: Add 10% tax.
Output: $11.

Easy, right?

But what if the rules are too complicated to write by hand?

Think about recognizing a cat in a photo. 
How would you write exact code for whiskers, ears, and shadows in every possible lighting condition?

It's almost impossible.

This is where Machine Learning flips the formula. 🤖

Instead of writing rules, you give the computer:
1. Data (thousands of animal photos)
2. Answers ("this is a cat", "this is a dog")

And the computer figures out the **Rules** on its own!

It looks for patterns.
It learns from its mistakes.
And it gets smarter over time.

When I first studied this at the University of Lahore, this simple mental shift blew my mind. 💡

We aren't writing the logic anymore. 
We are teaching the machine to discover it.

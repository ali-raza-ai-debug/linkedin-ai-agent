As I dive deeper into Deep Learning from my desk here in Pakistan, I am learning that the most dangerous bugs do not crash your program.

They are completely silent.

Here are two common mistakes beginners make when training neural networks:

1. Forgetting to clear gradients

In PyTorch, if you forget to call zero_grad before your backward pass, gradients accumulate from the previous step. 

Your code runs smoothly, but your updates are completely ruined.

2. The weight symmetry trap

If you initialize all your weights to zero, every neuron does the exact same thing during backpropagation.

They learn together and update together. 

Instead of learning complex features, your network just duplicates the same output.

What actually powers ChatGPT under the hood?

As I continue my journey of learning LLMs in public, one core idea keeps coming up everywhere. 

It is the Transformer architecture.

Before this came along, older models processed text sequentially, step by step. That made it tricky to handle long text efficiently.

Transformers changed the game with a concept called self-attention.

This mechanism allows the model to process sequences while capturing long-range context efficiently. 

Instead of looking at words in isolation, it understands how words relate to each other across an entire piece of text. 

By using multi-head attention and feed-forward networks, Transformers handle sequential data with remarkable efficiency.

Today, variations of this architecture serve as the foundation for modern generative AI, powering tools like ChatGPT, Claude, and BERT.

I am still studying the details, but it is amazing to see how one architectural shift unlocked the AI boom we see today.

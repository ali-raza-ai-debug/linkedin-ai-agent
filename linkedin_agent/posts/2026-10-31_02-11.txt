The biggest shift in machine learning is unlearning traditional coding.

When I first started, I was used to writing explicit rules.

If X happens, do Y. Step by step.

Machine learning flips that idea completely on its head.

Instead of hardcoding every single rule, we feed historical data to the computer.

The machine then studies the data and discovers the patterns on its own.

According to IBM, machine learning is already a $21 billion industry, projected to grow to $209 billion by 2029.

With that kind of demand, it is easy to feel rushed to jump straight into complex models.

But for me, taking the time to understand this simple mindset shift made all the difference.

Foundations first. Everything else builds on top of that. 💡

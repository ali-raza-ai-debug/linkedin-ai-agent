Machine learning really boils down to two main ways of learning.

One has a teacher. The other does not. 🤖

Here is how I like to think about supervised vs unsupervised learning:

1. Supervised Learning

You give the model both the questions and the answers.

Think of flashcards. You show it thousands of photos labeled "cat" or "dog".

The model learns the pattern between the input data and the correct label.

Later, you show it a new photo, and it predicts what it is.

Common examples: Spam detection, predicting prices, image classification.

2. Unsupervised Learning

You give the model raw data without any labels or answers.

Imagine handing a child a box of mixed Lego bricks.

Without any instructions, they might start grouping them by color or size.

That is unsupervised learning. The model discovers hidden patterns and clusters completely on its own.

Common examples: Customer grouping, anomaly detection, recommendation systems.

Revisiting these basics always helps me connect the dots better as I keep learning and building.

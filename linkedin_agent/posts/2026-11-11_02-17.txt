AI does not see words the way you and I do.

When I started diving deeper into LLMs, this concept was a huge lightbulb moment for me.

Large language models do not process whole sentences at once. 

Instead, they break text down into small building blocks called tokens.

A token can be a single character, a partial word, or a full word. 

Even spaces and punctuation marks count as tokens! 🧩

Here is a quick rule of thumb for the math:

In English, 1 token is roughly 4 characters, or about 0.75 words. 

So 100 tokens translate to about 75 words.

This is why AI context windows are measured in tokens rather than words. 

For example, a 1-million-token context window can process roughly 750,000 words. 

That is the equivalent of feeding the AI about ten novels at once! 📚

Understanding tokens makes working with AI models and costs so much clearer.

Did you know that spaces and punctuation counted as tokens when you first learned about AI?

Why do we hide data from our own Machine Learning models? 🤔

When I first began learning ML, splitting a dataset into training and test sets felt a bit confusing.

Why not train the model on all of our data to make it as smart as possible?

Here is what I learned. 💡

If a model sees all the data during training, it can simply memorize the answers instead of finding real patterns.

This causes train-test contamination, also known as data leakage.

When answers leak into the training process, your model gets artificially high accuracy scores. It looks amazing on paper, but it completely collapses on real-world data.

This is a much bigger issue than many realize.

In fact, a 2023 review revealed that data leakage impacted at least 294 academic publications across 17 scientific disciplines!

Splitting data keeps us honest. 🎯

We train the model on one portion and test it on a hidden portion it has never seen before. 

It is the only true way to check if our model actually learned, or if it just memorized the test.

I used to think LLMs and AI agents were the same thing.

As I continue my AI learning series, this was one of the first big concepts I had to clear up.

Here is the simplest way to understand the difference:

An LLM is like the brain.
An AI agent is the full system.

A Large Language Model (LLM) is a central reasoning engine. You give it a prompt, and it generates a response. It reacts to your input, answers your question, and stops there.

An AI agent takes that core reasoning engine and equips it with:
- Memory to remember context
- Planning to break down complex goals into smaller tasks
- External tools to take real actions

So while an LLM gives you an answer, an AI agent actually completes the workflow.

One generates text. The other takes action.

Understanding this difference has completely changed how I approach my learning every day.

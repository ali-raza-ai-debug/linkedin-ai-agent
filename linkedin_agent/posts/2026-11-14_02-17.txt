As I continue my journey learning LLMs, two key terms keep coming up: Embeddings and RAG.

Here is how I understand them in simple terms.

First, what are Embeddings?

AI models do not read words the way humans do. They understand numbers.

Embeddings convert text into numerical values, allowing the AI to search databases and measure which ideas are closest in meaning.

Second, what is RAG?

RAG stands for Retrieval-Augmented Generation.

Instead of relying only on what it learned in the past, RAG connects an AI directly to fresh, company-specific documents.

By turning text into embeddings, the system searches internal databases for the most relevant context before generating an answer.

This dramatically reduces inaccurate or outdated responses.

For example, companies like DoorDash use RAG chatbots to instantly pull delivery policies and improve customer support experiences. 📚

It is basically like giving the AI an open-book test instead of forcing it to answer from memory.

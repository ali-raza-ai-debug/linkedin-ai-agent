Is AI learning with a teacher, or figuring things out on its own? 💡

As I continue my Machine Learning learning series, I want to share how I easily break down these two core concepts.

1. Supervised Learning

The model trains on labeled data where the correct answers are already known. 

A great real-world example is your email inbox. Models are trained on historical messages labeled as "spam" or "not spam" to automatically catch junk mail before it reaches you.

2. Unsupervised Learning

The model receives raw, unlabeled data and uncovers hidden patterns on its own.

For instance, retailers use unsupervised clustering algorithms to group customers by shared purchasing habits without needing any predefined categories.

In simple terms: supervised learning learns from known answers, while unsupervised learning discovers hidden structures.

Both are incredibly useful depending on the problem you are trying to solve.

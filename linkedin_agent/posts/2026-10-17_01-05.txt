Traditional coding gives rules. Machine Learning finds them.

When I first started studying AI, I struggled to understand what Machine Learning actually meant behind all the jargon.

Here is the simplest way I learned to think about it:

In traditional programming, you give the computer:
1. The data
2. The exact rules

The computer follows your rules to give you the answer.

Machine Learning flips this completely 💡

You give the computer:
1. The data
2. The correct answers

Then, the computer figures out the hidden rules on its own.

Instead of writing thousands of manual "if-else" conditions to catch spam emails, you simply show the model 10,000 examples of spam and normal emails.

It studies the patterns and learns how to decide for itself 🤖

That small shift in mindset made a huge difference in my learning journey.
